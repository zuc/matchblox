What it uses, and what it does not

Bars.▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱: fill in the accent below 60 %, warn from 60 %, neg from 90 %. The percent takes the colour of its bar, except below 60 %.
Forecast.lasts the week in muted; out Fri ~20:00 in warn. One for each account.
The stoker word.stoker in the night token (#7C8BB0), only while it runs. Nothing else here moves; only the match does, on the Sessions faces and in the compact island.
Glyphs.▰ for the host, ╿ for a wait, ✦ only for a burning session. No glyph for a rest that is not a wait.
